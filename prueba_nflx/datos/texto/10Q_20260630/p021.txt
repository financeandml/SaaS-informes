10. Stockholders’ Equity
Equity Incentive Plans
The Netflix, Inc. 2020 Stock Plan is a stockholder-approved plan that provides for the grant of incentive stock options to employees and for the grant of
non-statutory stock options, stock appreciation rights, restricted stock and restricted stock units to employees, directors and consultants.
Stock Option Activity
Stock options are generally vested in full upon the grant date and are exercisable for the full ten-year contractual term regardless of employment status.

Restricted Stock Unit Activity
The Company grants time-based restricted stock unit (“RSU”) awards and performance-based restricted stock unit (“PSU”) awards to certain executive
officers. RSU awards vest quarterly over a three-year period subject to the executive’s continued employment or service with the Company through the vesting
date. PSU awards have performance periods ranging from one to three years and vest depending on the Company’s achievement of predetermined market￾based performance targets.
