Why this director is valuable to Netflix 
Ms. Sweeney has held various senior positions with large entertainment companies, which 
provided her with broad strategic and operational experience. Her experience in the 
entertainment industry provides a unique business perspective to the Board as Netflix builds its 
global internet TV network. 
Also… 
Ms. Sweeney’s entertainment experience spans more than three decades, including her 
oversight of Disney’s cable, broadcast and satellite properties globally for 18 years. During that 
time, she was charged with launching and running over 118 Disney Channels in 164 countries 
in 34 languages, and had oversight over various ABC properties including ABC Television 
Network, ABC Studios and the Disney ABC Cable Networks Group. Prior to Disney, she was 
CEO of FX Networks, Inc. from 1993 to 1996 and spent more than 12 years at Viacom’s 
Nickelodeon Network. She holds an Ed. M. From Harvard University and a B.A. from the College 
of New Rochelle. 
Career Snapshot:
