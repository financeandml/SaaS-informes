Required Vote 
We have implemented majority voting in uncontested elections of directors. Our Bylaws provide that in an uncontested election, 
each director will be elected by a vote of the majority votes cast, which includes votes to withhold authority but excludes 
abstentions. Abstentions and broker non-votes are not counted as votes cast and therefore will have no effect on determining 
whether the required majority vote has been attained.
