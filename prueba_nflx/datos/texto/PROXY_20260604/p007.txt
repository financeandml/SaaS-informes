87 INFORMATION CONCERNING 
SOLICITATION AND VOTING 
On November 14, 2025, the Company effected a ten-for-one forward stock split of its common stock (the “Stock Split”) with a 
proportionate increase in the number of shares of authorized common stock. All references made to common stock, equity award 
and per share amounts throughout this proxy statement have been retroactively adjusted to reflect the Stock Split.
